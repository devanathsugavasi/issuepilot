# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the
work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not tolerated in issues, pull
requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/devanathsugavasi/issuepilot/security/advisories/new)
marked "Code of conduct" (this keeps the report non-public), or by contacting a maintainer
directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove content and restrict
participation for violations, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
